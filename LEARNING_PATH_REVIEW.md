# REI learning journey and seminar alignment

The supplied seminarrrr.docx establishes the conceptual sequence. The user's final brief changes the delivery: REI is primarily for continued independent work afterward, gives learning and project users equal entry, and publishes no event dates, venue, attendee lists, committee names or registration information. The source document remains unmodified and is not included in the public site.

## Seminar to product map

| Seminar content | Learning step | What the learner writes | Deeper existing material |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Opening research mindset | Begin with a purpose | Observation, purpose, assumption to challenge | Lesson 1 and idea notebook |
| Phase 1 meaningful ideas | Shape your question | Topic, evidenced problem, question, scope | Lessons 2 and 6 |
| Phase 2 search and evaluation | Find credible sources | Query, source details, evaluation, next search | Lesson 3, project Library and search log |
| Phase 3 literature synthesis | Connect what studies show | Comparison, synthesis, provisional gap | Lessons 4–5, Evidence and Synthesis |
| Phase 4 problems evidence solutions | Build a research blueprint | Objective, preliminary framework, collection/analysis, ethics/limits | Lessons 7–8 and project Design |
| Phase 5 integration and open-forum questions | Review and keep improving | A transferable lesson, feedback question, next action | Lesson 9, workbook and project Review |

The six steps contain 21 prompts. They produce a preliminary blueprint, not completed institutional Chapters 1–4 or an approved study. Participants can use seminar notes; independent learners need no seminar attendance.

## Extra practice and its evidence

Eight original activities cover recall, question repair, source investigation, synthesis/disagreement, claim boundaries, method alignment, feedback/revision and spaced revisiting. Learners write before seeing a comparison answer; two scenarios also provide choice-specific explanations. Revisions and recorded review visits persist. There is no automatic evaluation of open writing or research quality.

The design rationale is exposed in #learning-evidence and in each activity's optional evidence note. Studies inform the mechanism; the REI exercises are adaptations and have not themselves been tested.

- Lazonder and Harmsen (2016), a synthesis of 72 inquiry studies, informs optional guidance and manageable task steps. https://doi.org/10.3102/0034654315627366
- Karpicke and Blunt (2011), experiments on learning science texts, informs writing an explanation before consulting guidance. This is not direct evidence of improved proposal-writing skill. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21252317/
- McGrew (2020), a pre/post study with 68 eleventh-grade students, informs investigating provenance, evidence and outside corroboration. Scholarly method appraisal needs additional judgment. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2019.103711
- Rittle-Johnson and Star (2009), an experiment with 162 school students learning equations, informs comparing alternatives and explaining differences. Transfer to research design is an adaptation. https://doi.org/10.1037/a0014224
- Cho and MacArthur (2010), a small undergraduate writing study, informs specific feedback and deliberate revision. REI does not provide human peer review or send work to others. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2009.08.006
- Dunlosky and colleagues (2013), a learning-techniques review, informs retrieval and distributed revisiting. REI does not infer mastery or prescribe an optimal revisit interval. https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266

Source findings, populations and limitations were checked against publisher abstracts or university research records. These references support design decisions; no REI learning-effect estimate is claimed. Expert review, user research and an appropriate educational evaluation remain future work.
