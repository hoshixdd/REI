# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Inferred from the repository and the user's briefs. Not a fresh interview.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing vanilla hash-router SPA in `dist/`. Local-first. Do not replace the stack.

## Users

A person learning how to turn a question into a research proposal. They work in this browser, often alone, and return to unfinished drafts.

## Product Purpose

REI teaches a nine-lesson method and keeps the work that comes out of it: notes, lesson drafts, sources, evidence, claims, and a proposal. Success is a clearer argument the person can keep editing, not a score.

## Positioning

The evidence chain stays visible and local: question, claim, evidence note, passage, source, limitation. No required account, no automatic scientific judgment.

## Brand commitments

- Name and wordmark: REI.
- The restored moonlight room is the identity: near-black field, cool silver type, ice accent. Not lime, not copper, not a purple glow.
- Display italics use the existing Instrument Serif file because it is already the product's lettering, not a new default.
- Routes, lesson content, and local saves stay.

## Constraints

- Data stays in localStorage and IndexedDB.
