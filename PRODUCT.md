# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Inferred from the repository and the user's briefs. Not a fresh interview.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing vanilla hash-router SPA in `dist/`. Local-first. Do not replace the stack.

## Users

A person learning how to turn a question into a research proposal. They work in this browser, often alone, and return to unfinished drafts.

## Product Purpose

REI teaches a nine-lesson method and keeps the work that comes out of it: notes, lesson drafts, sources, evidence, claims, and a proposal. Success is a clearer argument the person can keep editing, not a score.

## Positioning

The evidence chain stays visible and local: question, claim, evidence note, passage, source, limitation. No required account, no automatic scientific judgment.

## Brand commitments

- Name and wordmark: REI.
- The restored moonlight room is the identity: near-black field, cool silver type, ice accent. Not lime, not copper, not a purple glow.
- Display italics use the existing Instrument Serif file because it is already the product's lettering, not a new default.
- Routes, lesson content, and local saves stay.

## Constraints

- Data stays in localStorage and IndexedDB.
- Production: https://rei-pink.vercel.app/

## Continued learning experience

The product primarily supports independent work after the writeshop, while giving new independent learners and active research-project users equal entry. The six-step learning path reflects the seminar's opening and five phases without event logistics or dates. Eight research-informed practice activities complement the existing nine lessons. A personal workbook connects the guided prompts and revisions. Learning evidence and transfer limits are visible; the product has no measured educational-impact claim.

The brief welcome at the bare URL/#welcome leads into #home, the original optical homepage. The actual homepage integrates the six-step workshop sequence, learning/project starting choices, practice activities and workbook access. Its optical story links to matching learning steps. #explore remains an alias; #learn is the detailed learning dashboard. Keep existing project records, lesson drafts, local backups and the established optical identity intact. Guided workbook text does not silently overwrite project proposals or lesson originals.
